---
name: VitalLens report evidence
description: Product rules for what post-measurement reports may display and claim.
---

VitalLens reports describe one completed measurement at a time. Keep fingertip PPG and face-camera reports separate. Show only values returned or estimated by that modality for that session; omit unavailable metrics rather than filling them in. Fingertip PPG may include quality-gated pulse-interval variability such as SDNN, RMSSD, and pNN50, but label it as optical pulse-to-pulse data, not ECG R-R data. Do not invent scores or claim stress, energy, health status, diagnosis, or population comparisons.

**Why:** The two measurement paths expose different and incomplete signals, so combining sessions or presenting camera-derived variability as ECG-equivalent would misrepresent the measurement.

**How to apply:** Check report metric lists, summaries, and future report features against the active session's source and actual returned values. Withhold fingertip pulse-interval metrics when the camera signal does not meet the estimator's quality requirements.
